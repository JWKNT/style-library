# Tessera visual inspection

All 26 letters were inspected in full-alphabet rendered sheets at 300, 120, and 96 pixels high. The final O and Q were inspected again individually after making their small outer jewels mirror each other. Reverse artwork on a dark teal field was inspected for clear counters and genuinely transparent inlays.

- A: Clear apex, open counter, strong crossbar, balanced splayed terminals.
- B: Distinct upper and lower faceted bowls; both counters remain open.
- C: Open aperture and balanced forked ends; no collision.
- D: Strong vertical stem and distinct faceted counter.
- E: Three separate bars; shorter center arm is clear.
- F: Clear upper and middle arms with separate terminal ornament.
- G: Open upper aperture and strong inward bar distinguish it from C.
- H: Clear gap above and below crossbar; split terminals consistent.
- I: Compact bilateral caps distinguish it from a bare stem.
- J: Clear lower hook and upper cap; terminals remain within viewBox.
- K: Both diagonal arms and stem remain distinct at their junction.
- L: Wide foot and lifted terminal; open upper-right field.
- M: Deep center notch and separately visible diagonal legs.
- N: Strong ascending diagonal, clear side bearings.
- O: One large clear counter; paired small outer jewels are balanced.
- P: One open bowl counter, extended stem.
- Q: Clear O structure with a separate descending diagonal tail; no clipping.
- R: One bowl counter and clear diagonal leg.
- S: Open opposing apertures and faceted waist remain readable.
- T: Wide cap with distinct centered stem.
- U: Open top and faceted rounded base.
- V: Wide opening and flat pointed base clearly distinct from U.
- W: Double trough and raised middle joint; readable at 96px.
- X: Crossed diagonals remain clear despite the center inlays.
- Y: Clear fork and single descending stem.
- Z: Strong diagonal connecting parallel top and bottom bars.

The geometric inlay details intentionally simplify at 96px; the silhouette and letter recognition remain strong. No alternate-color paint is used to imitate holes. Every SVG is a single even-odd currentColor path with a 44-unit safety margin. Raster checks independently confirmed all expected large counters, transparent backgrounds, black ink, and unclipped bounds at all three proof sizes.
