# Visual review

All 23 full-size generated-font proofs were opened and inspected. The proof text
covers uppercase, lowercase, digits, punctuation, short text, kerning pairs, and
paragraphs at 28–32 px. The contact sheets were also compared across families.

The review found and corrected two reading-size issues:

- The initial italic i fitting made its stem too dark, and a general serif rule
  incorrectly treated short dot strokes as letter terminals. Narrow-stem fitting
  is now capped, and dots are excluded from the serif treatment.
- The initial looped l in selected faces resembled a parenthesis in words such as
  “falls.” It now has a clear upright stem, with an independently drawn exit or
  subtle stem curve.

A deeper contour test found integer-rounding self-touches in some generated
outlines. Geometry is now quantized on the integer grid before contours are
emitted. Every tested custom ASCII contour is simple after this correction.

## Family review

- Wain Script: flowing lowercase, stacked-loop g, and clear narrow stems.
- Waverly Quill: angular shoulders and open g remain readable in paragraphs.
- Waxwing Italic: dark nib strokes retain open counters and clear digits.
- Wayfarer Book: modest slant, visible slab ends, and steady word spacing.
- Westlake Flourish: long Q tail stays below the baseline without clipping.
- Whitethorn Nib: distinct cut terminals and a readable stacked-loop g.
- Wildmere Grace: airy construction and pointed shoulders remain legible.
- Willowmark Letter: heavier rounded pen is balanced by large open counters.
- Windharp Calligraphic: fine diagonal movement remains readable at proof size.
- Woad Engraved: angular bowls and fine hairlines are clear in display text.
- Wycliff Stone: broader stone-cut forms and serif feet remain distinct.
- Wyrtle Slab: open lowercase and numerals retain a firm, medium-weight rhythm.
- Xanthic Facet: chamfered forms read clearly beside curved punctuation.
- Xebec Stencil: deliberate horizontal bridges retain recognizable letters.
- Xenia Round: soft terminals, rounded bowls, and open apertures are intact.
- Xeric Grotesk: compact rectangular bowls have sufficient inner space.
- Xylo Play: ball terminals and angular shoulders create a playful text texture.
- Yarrow Poster: heavy lettering remains open at paragraph proof size.
- Yestrel Ribbon: broad diagonal stress and small fold cuts remain coherent.
- Yonder Deco: low crossbars, narrow capitals, and thin horizontals are intact.
- Zephyr Tilt: light outlines and clear l stems retain an open reading rhythm.
- Zinnia Soft: rounded slab ends are clear and counters stay open.
- Zorin Inlay: internal channels are clear at display size; small text is legible,
  though this decorative face is best used for short text and headings.

All proof content fits the canvas. The stencil and inline faces are deliberate
display styles; the proofs do not imply they are optimized for long body copy.
