JWKNT Style Library: Nocturne, Obelisk, Cameo

The new decorative compositions, original ornament geometry, and generator are
provided under CC0 1.0 Universal (CC0-1.0.txt). These are illustrations, not font
programs. No font downloads, text elements, raster images, or embedded fonts are
needed to render the SVG files. All paper-colored details are genuinely transparent.

The Readers Rothenburg, Gotische and Royal illustrations were inspected as visual

They therefore are not source components of these new alphabets.
