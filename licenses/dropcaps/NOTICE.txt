Style Library decorative initials

Ten complete A–Z SVG illustration alphabets, prepared in 2026. The files contain outlines and ornament. They do not load fonts or other resources.

Thornwood, Scriptorium, and Ribbon are modified illustrations based on Morris Initialen, Royal Initialen, and Carrick Caps, digitized by Dieter Steffmann / Typographer Mediengestaltung. The SVGs use new names and new decorative compositions. They are distributed under LPPL 1.3c or later. Exact source illustrations, original notices, modification records, and the LPPL text are in sources/dropcaps/medieval/. The complete unmodified CTAN collection is available at https://mirrors.ctan.org/fonts/initials.zip .

Iris, Obelisk, and Tessera use original letter construction. Laurel uses Noto Serif Display Regular outlines. Briar uses DejaVu Serif outlines. Nocturne uses Euler Bold outlines. Cameo uses Noto Serif Display Bold outlines. Each has new decorative artwork. Original contributions are under CC0 1.0. The retained source-font notices still apply to the source fonts and inherited material; they are not replaced by CC0.
